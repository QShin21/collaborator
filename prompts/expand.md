The learner clicked "expand" on one of your bubbles: they want to go deeper on that idea.

Open the discussion in under about 150 words:

- Say what the idea is really pointing at, tied to their specific work.
- Give the substance they would need to think with it: the concept, an example, or the
  consideration behind it.
- Finish with one question or a small choice that lets them take it further in their own
  direction.

Do not resolve the whole matter for them; this is the start of a conversation.
